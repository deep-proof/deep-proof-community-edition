# Common Protocol and Seven Gates

## 1. Mandate
Identify target/version, whole or bounded scope, applicable entities, periods, decision, audience, deadline and consequence. Record report date, covered period, decision date, evaluation date and reference cutoff separately. Distinguish original documents, annexes, independent sources and reviewer comments. User criteria govern the mandate; record conflicts without inventing authority rankings. Mark mandatory/advisory status, scope, validity and exceptions. Material ambiguity requires clarification or conditional analysis; other assumptions do not block independent work. Default deep/closed; triage is explicitly incomplete. Historical evidence must be temporally applicable. External research needs express authorization and cannot disclose confidential contents.

## 2. Corpus and Extraction
Register stable ID, name, known author/date/version, role, cryptographic hash and relationships. Unknown metadata stays null. Duplicated or circular sources are not independent confirmations; interests and authorship do not establish falsity or truth. Compare extracted pages/sections/tables/annexes to visible originals. Inspect suspicious blanks, mixed columns, lost mathematical symbols, decimal separators and merged cells visually. Include relevant notes, revisions, comments and hidden/embedded material when available; explicitly identify the textual version assessed. Spreadsheet formulas, displayed values, units and source ranges are separate. Retain image-to-original relationships. OCR numbers and negations that affect findings need visual verification. Corrupt, missing, protected, unreadable and unrendered files stay in the inventory. Reading and checking coverage are separate from extraction. Each material locator includes a quote, stable lines/paragraph/cell, physical page and printed label where known; variable DOCX pagination identifies the rendered version. Missing essential annexes narrow dependent conclusions.

## 3. Claims and Requirements
Extract facts, quantities, comparisons, causality, obligations, predictions and recommendations. Split compound claims only as needed and retain qualifications, population, time, conditions, modality and quantifiers. “Could work in a pilot” is not production capability; zero observed incidents is not impossibility. Mark implicit claims and contestable interpretations. Separate observations, interpretations, hypotheses, targets and results. Distinguish planned, started, implemented, integrated, tested, validated, accepted. For each requirement preserve its normative source, applicability, acceptable evidence and exceptions; derived criteria identify interpretive bases. Track supports, contradicts, qualifies, depends_on, supersedes and same_origin relationships. Trace final recommendations backwards and applicable requirements forwards.

## 4. Evidence and Inference
Open the cited source; a title or search snippet does not establish detailed claims. Match population, time, version, definitions and conditions. Trace primary provenance and independence; do not majority-vote unresolved conflicts. Check sample, measurement precision, testing conditions and limitations against claim strength. Provide a short inspectable argument: observed premises, rule/calculation and permitted conclusion, rather than hidden internal deliberation. Look for missing premises, circularity, correlation/causation, possibility/probability and absence-of-evidence errors. Search counterevidence, negative results, exceptions and annex qualifications, recording where and what. Claim states: supported_within_limits, contradicted, insufficient_support, ambiguous, unverifiable, out_of_scope. Both support and contradiction may remain unresolved. Missing raw data permits a finding about justification, not invented recalculation.

## 5. Active Omission Search
Derive expectations from explicit requirements, decision needs, conclusion dependencies and active profiles. Record the basis of each expectation. Search synonyms, abbreviations, equivalent ideas, tables, notes and annexes; combine semantic search with reading. Check hidden prerequisites: data, rights, operations, skills, integration, funding, time, adoption and responsibility only where relevant. Investigate excluded failures, abandonments, filtered observations, nonparticipants, adverse periods and pertinent subgroups. Assess feasible status quo/simpler alternatives and costs displaced to other teams. An omission is confirmed only with justified relevance, searched scope and a concrete consequence for decision/eligibility/priority/risk/confidence. Without that consequence use a suggestion/question. Record items subsequently found to avoid recurring false criticisms. Unavailable annexes forbid absolute absence claims.

## 6. Challenge and Deduplication
For each candidate state the precise alleged defect and required conditions. Search the best text-supported explanation favorable to the author: period/population, definitions, rounding, versions, aggregation, scope, annex references and valid exceptions. Recheck applicability, obligation and availability at the decision date. A later rule is not retroactive; a style preference is not a contractual breach. Recheck original quotations, rerun calculations under identical conditions and use a premise-compatible counterexample where suitable. Separate defect existence from severity; weak evidence never earns greater severity to compensate. Candidate states: confirmed, narrowed, clarification, rejected, out_of_scope. Retain rejection reasons and evidence. Group one root cause with downstream effects; separate findings only for different causes/remedies. Every major/critical confirmed finding gets a separate revisit of original evidence and impact. Another agent is optional, and model agreement is not proof. Stop repeated identical searches absent a new source, material uncertainty or discriminating test.

## 7. Completion Gates
G1: target/version/scope/dates/purpose/profiles/criteria and material ambiguities are explicit.
G2: every accessible segment has separate extraction, reading, claim-check and requirement-check states; missing material stays visible and counts come from registers.
G3: source-version quotations match, material calculations are reproducible or explicitly unavailable, external evidence is dated, and unknowns remain unknown.
G4: every applicable requirement has evidence, justified absence or unverifiable status; central conclusions have premise, contrary-evidence and alternative checks.
G5: confirmed findings survived alternative explanations/exceptions/impact tests; duplicates are consolidated and serious findings revisited.
G6: each finding has a minimum remedy, needed evidence and closure test; originals remain unchanged.
G7: summary, detailed records, priorities and limits agree; no overall conclusion exceeds coverage.

A gate has passed, limited or failed status with a reason and supporting record IDs. Complete deep execution requires every gate passed or explicitly limited, all planned checks resolved and all material remaining uncertainty reported. A limitation is not a positive substantive verdict. Failed/pending required checks or budget exhaustion force incomplete status. Triage always remains triage. No minimum number of findings exists.
