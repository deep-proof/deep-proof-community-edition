## Q01 — Mandate and Temporal Applicability
Assess the target identity, version, scope, decision question, audience, consequences, dates, authority conflicts and applicability of criteria. Separate contemporaneous judgment from later updates.

## Q02 — Corpus and Extraction Integrity
Assess source provenance, duplicates, original verification, text extraction, OCR uncertainty, version identity, locators and explicit coverage gaps.

## Q03 — Claims, Requirements and Dependencies
Assess material claims with their qualifications, the distinction between plans and results, applicable requirements and bidirectional traceability between requirements, evidence and conclusions.

## Q04 — Evidence and Inference
Assess citation fidelity, contextual relevance, source independence, method strength, logical transformations, counterevidence and justified local claim verdicts.

## Q05 — Material Omissions and Alternatives
Search justified expectations across accessible sources, prerequisites, exclusions, feasible alternatives and shifted costs. Demonstrate the contextual consequence of any omission.

## Q06 — Quantitative and Domain Checks
Reproduce material calculations, denominators, units, chronology and sensitivity where evidence permits. Apply every relevant domain profile without inventing standards or results.

## Q07 — Contested Findings and Proportionate Remedies
Challenge every candidate, consolidate root causes, justify severity separately from confidence and urgency, and specify minimum remedies and verifiable closure conditions.

## Q08 — Honest Coverage and Decision Implications
Align summary, findings and scope; disclose missing evidence, incomplete work and unknowns. Permit zero confirmed findings and avoid certification or exhaustive assurance claims.
