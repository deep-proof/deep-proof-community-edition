# Domain Profiles

Activate every applicable profile and record why; mixed reports require more than one. Apply the common protocol to the whole scoped target. These checks do not themselves create legal, contractual or scientific obligations. Missing domain inputs narrow verification rather than license invented facts.

## scientific — Scientific and Experimental Reports
Reconstruct hypothesis, variables, observation unit, population and primary outcome; compare measurement to declared purpose and distinguish exploratory from confirmatory analysis. Examine selection, comparators, allocation, confounding, observation dependence and experimental conditions. Causal attribution needs justified assumptions, not merely an association. Compare initial and analyzed samples; inspect post-hoc exclusions, missing observations, duplicates, instrument changes and undisclosed transformations without inferring fraud from an isolated anomaly. Check statistical assumptions, multiple comparisons, effect size, uncertainty, robustness and practical relevance. Nonsignificance is not equivalence or no effect. Compare studied populations/environments to generalization, including convenient samples, ideal conditions and surrogate outcomes. Declared limitations do not excuse an unconditional abstract. Reproduce only with supplied data/code/resources and authorization, record versions/environment differences, and do not interpret environmental failure alone as invalidity. Search omitted negative findings, shifted endpoints, favorable subgroup selection, outdated comparators, contradictions across abstract/results/limitations and unsupported novelty. A causal conclusion beyond observational evidence needs narrowing or a discriminating test; an uncontrolled confounder is a possible explanation, not an established cause.

## ai — AI Evaluations and Benchmarks
Identify model/system versions, prompts, tools, retries, temperature, compute and human interventions; distinguish model from complete system. Audit dataset origin, declared rights, representativeness, difficulty, train/tuning/test separation, duplicates and test-set adaptation. Unknown training data makes contamination a risk/unknown, not a proved fact. Audit labels, ambiguity, evaluator competence and disagreement adjudication. For model judges check prompts, ordering/style preference and independent calibration; judge agreement does not guarantee correctness. Reproduce denominators, thresholds, precision/recall, abstention, coverage, subgroup/tail/rare-consequence performance and error costs. Equal comparisons need equivalent data, tools, information, resources, attempts and stopping rules. Compare per-attempt and per-accepted-answer cost, including queues, tools, retries, review and human labor. Inspect run variation, reformulation sensitivity, distribution shifts, incomplete inputs and uncertainty; agent evaluations also check effects and error recovery. Active security tests need authorization. Distinguish empirical finite tests from formal guarantees and identify exactly what “verified” or “deterministic” covers. Representation validity does not establish fidelity to language or completeness of requirements. Search best-run selection, discarded timeouts, tuned benchmarks, proxy utility, missing simple baselines and universal safety claims.

## progress — Project Progress, Deliverables and Funded Projects
Extract applicable objectives, work packages, tasks, deliverables, indicators, responsibilities, deadlines and approved changes. Separate binding commitments from indicative plans. Map each commitment to reported work, observable result, source and separate planned/started/implemented/integrated/tested/validated/accepted states. Formal acceptance needs appropriate authority/evidence. Compare periods for disappeared work, repeated deferrals, renamed terms, recycled results and responsibility changes; inspect explanations first. Distinguish consortium results, organization contribution, integration and original development; legitimate integration is still a contribution if attributed accurately. Trace dependencies and whether delayed components contradict overall completion; justified deferral describes cause, effect, revised plan and downstream consequences. Recalculate indicators, definitions and periods; outputs are not adoption/impact, a demo is not operational validation, and counts alone are not quality. Compare revised/original reports for lost still-valid status, limitations, evidence, dependencies and responsibility. Read program-specific requirements from applicable sources, never memory.

## decision — Decisions, Strategy and Selection
Identify approval, decision authority, objectives, hard constraints, horizon and reversibility. Separate directional recommendation, spending authorization and implementation approval; do not judge a preliminary memo as a final authorization. Reconstruct feasible alternatives and realistic status quo; do not propose an ideal alternative that ignores budget, time or skills. Extract weights, thresholds, eliminators and aggregation; inspect post-hoc criteria, correlated double-counting and impermissible compensation of hard constraints. Compare options on equal conditions and evidence; distinguish full cost from purchase price, observations from vendor estimates and analyst assumptions. Vary justified weights/estimates, recompute rankings and identify switching assumptions and value of more information. A sensitive preference may be rational if managed explicitly. Check implementation prerequisites appropriate to the phase: owners, resources, dependencies, deadlines, stopping signals and reversibility. Identify what would change the recommendation and whether it survives removal of speculative benefits. Preference disagreement is a trade-off, not a factual error; minimum remedy may be explicit acknowledgement rather than automatic reversal.

## commercial — Commercial, Financial and Market Reports
Identify offering, paying customer, sold unit, channel, adoption, pricing and serving cost. Separate users, active accounts, paying customers, contracted revenue and cash received. Reproduce market sizing with source/geography/segment/date, overlapping segments and accessible opportunity; market size alone proves neither channel nor willingness to pay. Distinguish exploratory discussions, interest letters, paid pilots, contracts and repeat use; inspect retention, conversion and concentration. Competitor funding is context, not evidence of profitability or demand for the target. Recompute variable costs, margin, acquisition cost and payback with retention assumptions; relevant AI economics include inference, retry, verification, support, storage and human work. Reconcile assumptions/revenue/expenses/cash; distinguish profit/liquidity and one-off/recurring expenditure. Use supplied/applicable accounting and tax rules with currencies, conversion dates and periods; do not infer treatments. Test delayed adoption, lower prices, higher costs, key-customer loss and supplier dependence; label scenarios and simultaneous optimism. For changing prices/competitors/funding use dated external sources only if authorized, otherwise record currentness unknown. This review is not a certified financial audit.

## technical — Technical Architecture and Feasibility
Extract operational functional/volume/latency/availability/environment/acceptance constraints; define vague scalability/robustness/real-time claims proportionately. Reconstruct components, interfaces, flows, states, dependencies and responsibilities; compare diagrams, prose and supplied code/config versions, respecting conceptual design scope. Recompute resource/capacity/data/latency budgets; check circular dependencies, incompatible assumptions and bottlenecks. A local benchmark does not establish distributed behavior where coordination dominates. Inspect timeout/retry/idempotency/concurrency/consistency/provider outage/recovery mechanisms and guarantee preconditions. Map material requirements to negative/boundary/integration tests; a passing test establishes its tested conditions only, and redundant tests are not independent coverage. Inspect/execute supplied code only within mandate and isolated authorization; no production changes, and absent code does not support alleged concrete implementation bugs. Check observability, migration, compatibility, rollback, external dependence, recurring cost and ownership appropriate to prototype versus production decisions. Remedies identify the minimum discriminating test or necessary change.

## security — Security, Privacy and Risk
Identify assets, actors, trust boundaries, adversary capabilities and consequences. For privacy map data, recipients, purpose, retention and third-party flows. Decompose general safety into evaluable properties. Compare threat-model coverage to architecture, exposed surfaces, privileges, keys, backups, logs, configuration and human processes. Excluded components may limit global conclusions. Map each control to threat/mechanism/configuration/operating proof/residual risk; policy existence is not enforcement, and transport encryption does not protect every copy or prevent technically authorized recipients reading data. Inspect test scope/duration/scenarios/limits: no discovered vulnerability is not no vulnerability. Confirm a vulnerability only with demonstrated preconditions and consequence in authorized scope. Separate possibility, exposure, probability estimate and impact; inspect score basis, compensating controls and exploitation cost without invented precision. Distinguish removed direct identifiers, pseudonymization and claimed anonymity; consider stated auxiliary data without identifying real persons or claiming unproved reidentification. Use versioned jurisdiction/period-specific norms; no generic certification. Reading a report authorizes neither attacks/payloads/third-party access nor data disclosure. Escalate specialist interpretation as an evidence-backed question.

## audit — Audit, Compliance and Eligibility
Fix the applicable regulation/contract/policy/standard/procedure, version, validity, entity, jurisdiction, activity and derogations. A thematically related rule cannot establish noncompliance. Turn each obligation into applicability, required evidence, method and allowed outcome. Distinguish doing an activity from documenting it; missing documentation need not prove nonperformance. Trace every conclusion through criterion/sample/source/result, and test whether sampling licenses extrapolation or universal claims. Inspect approvals, exceptions, transition periods, thresholds and accepted remediation with authority and bounded applicability. Separate established noncompliance, observation, risk, missing document and recommendation; tone does not determine gravity, and absent versus ineffective control may need different remedies. Map issue to declared corrective action and actual outcome; an updated procedure does not show enforcement. Closure needs its criterion and appropriate retesting/recurrence observation. Look for ignored exculpatory evidence, asymmetric similar cases and exceeded audit mandate; conflicts of interest alone do not falsify findings. Missing normative sources remain unknown, never guessed.

## incident — Incidents, Operations and Root Causes
Reconstruct event/log/notification/intervention/ticket chronology, normalize time zones and distinguish occurrence/detection/registration. Identify gaps and unsynchronized clocks before causal ordering. Separate symptoms, triggers, enabling conditions, causes and effects; preceding change is a hypothesis until discriminating evidence. Recompute duration, affected population, lost transactions and consequences, distinguishing total outage/degradation and potential/confirmed impact; inspect double counting and omitted periods. For each causal link test evidence, necessary conditions and alternatives, and whether removing the proposed cause would prevent the incident under observed conditions. Safe conceptual/isolated experiments do not authorize production recreation. Examine unclear procedures, ignored signals, monitoring/capacity limits and ownership rather than stopping at “human error.” Link corrective actions to causes or reduced impact, owner/deadline/effectiveness test/new risk. Restart is recovery, not necessarily cause elimination. Verify post-remediation operation, alerts and similar cases; short nonrecurrence proves only observed duration/conditions. Preserve competing causes when evidence does not discriminate.

## review — Reviews, Audits and Cross-Report Verification
Separate original documents, original evaluator criteria and reviewer findings; claims about report contents use the report, claims about reality may need independent evidence. Revalidate every existing criticism's quote, criterion, reasoning, severity and remedy; search decontextualized quotes, annex clarifications, asymmetry and exaggeration. Independently review originals using the common protocol rather than anchoring on the reviewer's list. Map independent issues to reviewer coverage: covered_correctly, covered_partially, omitted, unjustified_criticism, unverifiable. Omitted applies only to an issue in the original reviewer's scope and then-available material. Compare cross-document definitions/numbers/status/owners/deadlines/conclusions with explained changes, superseding estimates and dependent decision impacts. Review synthesis for lost uncertainty, negative results and contextual differences, and source-independent consensus; averaging opinions does not resolve factual incompatibility. Track old criticism/change/result across versions; legitimate claim narrowing can fix overstatement without new evidence, but inspect introduced inconsistencies and lost useful information. Separate original-document defects from review defects; do not automatically accuse the prior evaluator of negligence. Produce the review-coverage matrix.
