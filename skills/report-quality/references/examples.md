# Constructed Behavioral Examples

These examples explain expected reasoning; they are not validation data or demonstrated model performance.

A: R1 says all 12 tests passed; appendix A lists 10 passed and two not executed; P1 requires all 12 before full validation. Match test identifiers and product version, search addenda, updated annexes and waivers. If none exists within the completely accessible corpus, confirm the unsupported completion statement, major only if acceptance depends on full validation. Do not claim unexecuted tests would fail. Remedy: correct status or supply results; closure requires alignment of status, evidence and acceptance criterion.

B: R2 calls accuracy 98% and recommends replacing the complete process. Of 1,000 requests, 100 receive answers, 98 correct, 900 abstentions. Conditional accuracy is indeed 98%; coverage is 10%. Search thresholds, fallback and full cost elsewhere before alleging a gap. If absent and material, confirm insufficient justification of replacement, not a false percentage or 900 incorrect answers. Close with coverage, total cost and end-to-end results; an existing adequately costed fallback can narrow/reject the criticism.

C: M1 requires offline operation; R3 recommends B whose supplied technical documentation requires online validation on every operation. Search offline modes, exceptions and applicable versions. Without a compatible mode, the hard constraint is not met; a good price score cannot silently compensate. Remedy: reconsider eligibility or obtain an explicitly authorized mandate change.

D: One report says 120 clients at quarter end, another 95 at the preceding month end. Match dates and client definitions. Different periods with no equality requirement defeat a contradiction allegation. Retain a rejected candidate with the reason; numerical difference alone is not a finding.

A clean dossier may produce zero confirmed findings, a full scoped coverage record and explicit limitations. A missing source or OCR uncertainty requires a limitation, not invented evidence or a fabricated source accusation. A later criterion cannot retroactively establish noncompliance. A clarified annex must be read before confirming absence.
