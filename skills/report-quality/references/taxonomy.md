# Defect Taxonomy and Priorities

| Code | Defect and Minimum Discriminating Test |
| --- | --- |
| F01 | Contradicted fact: match entity, property, period, conditions and version before comparing contrary passages. |
| F02 | Insufficient support: identify evidence needed, evidence available and the exact gap; restrict the claim to justification. |
| F03 | Invalid/incomplete inference: state premises and the unsupported transformation or missing condition. |
| F04 | Material omission: establish a justified expectation, search the entire accessible relevant scope and explain consequence. |
| F05 | Unmet requirement: cite applicable obligation, evidence of failure and the check for a valid exception. |
| F06 | Numerical/dimensional error: reproduce inputs, formula, units, denominators and declared rounding tolerance. |
| F07 | Method/measurement defect: explain how design can distort results or cannot license the stated conclusion. |
| F08 | Citation/provenance defect: verify attribution, primary origin, context and source independence. |
| F09 | Version inconsistency: locate change and investigate explanation, redefinition or approval. |
| F10 | Material ambiguity: give two plausible readings and show different consequential judgments/actions. |
| F11 | Unfair comparison/selection: test equal criteria, conditions, resources, populations and horizons. |
| F12 | Insufficiently grounded decision: connect choice to objectives, constraints, feasible alternatives and sensitive assumptions. |

A lexical/statistical signal is only a candidate. Style preferences, irrelevant details, nonexistent standards and information present in accessible annexes are not material defects. Use multiple labels for one root cause without counting it multiple times.

Severity: critical invalidates a central decision or essential acceptance condition with an argued major consequence; major changes an important conclusion or requires substantial reanalysis; moderate affects local interpretation/verifiability without demonstrated global impact; minor is a real defect with bounded effect. Category alone never determines severity. Urgency (immediate, before_decision, scheduled) has its own contextual rationale. Confidence is high for direct reproducible evidence without an unresolved material conflict, medium for a solid inference with an explicit limitation, low for a question/investigation candidate. Do not confirm a low-confidence allegation as a firm defect; never invent certainty percentages. A disputed preference is not automatically wrong, and outcomes do not retrospectively determine decision reasonableness.
