# Evidence Review Report

<!-- section: executive-assessment -->
## Executive Assessment

Provide a concise, cited decision summary. Explain the most consequential findings and the limits of the evidence. Do not invent a decision threshold or recommendation unsupported by the dossier.

<!-- role: criteria -->
<!-- section: criterion-review -->
## Criterion-by-Criterion Review

Assess every ID from criteria.json exactly once. For each criterion, use its criterion marker, a short level-three heading, one assessment marker, and a cited explanation of the finding and its uncertainty. Do not assess this template's instructions as criteria.

<!-- section: findings -->
## Findings

Present evidence-grounded observations, grouping related findings by decision impact. Distinguish source claims from independent corroboration. Use cited prose or tables. Include a chart only when it clarifies supported data.

<!-- section: risks -->
## Risks

Explain the potential consequence of each supported risk and the evidence that motivates it. Separate estimated impact from confidence. Do not infer misconduct or assign numerical probabilities without support.

<!-- section: contradictions -->
## Contradictions

Compare conflicting assertions with citations to both sides. Reconcile period, currency, scope, and definitions where possible. State what cannot be reconciled from the available evidence.

<!-- section: missing-evidence -->
## Missing Evidence

Identify missing records and why they matter to the decision. Cite the existing claim or context that requires support. Limit absence claims to the reviewed collection.

<!-- section: questions-to-ask -->
## Questions To Ask

Propose prioritized questions tied to specific findings or unresolved criteria, with citations to the context. Explain what each answer would clarify. Use prose or a table, not a numbered or bulleted list.

<!-- section: recommendations -->
## Recommendations

Propose actions that follow from cited findings and risks. Explain their purpose and priority. Label proposed timing or responsible roles as proposals unless the sources specify them.

<!-- section: confidence-and-uncertainty -->
## Confidence And Uncertainty

Summarize material limits, competing explanations, and conclusions sensitive to missing evidence. Explain confidence in the findings rather than certainty that evidence is absent. Cite the underlying evidence and unresolved issues.
